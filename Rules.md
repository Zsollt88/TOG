##Towns of G'yul##

##GOLDEN RULE##
If a card's ability would conflict with the below rules ALWAYS the card's text wins!

##OVERVIEW##

There are two decks of 30 cards.
There are multiple goals:
- reach 50 victory points (VP) OR
- discard the opponent's deck

Draw pile:
A stack of cards which are not used and not discarded currently in the game. If a player can not draw cards from his draw pile at the START of his UPKEEP phase, this player loses the game.

Discard pile: 
A stack of card which are played or used in the game, but they are discarded by various reasons. They are not part of the draw pile and the play are anymore, however there can be abilities which can come back cards from the discard pile.

Cards:
Type of cards:
- Buildings (core structures of reaching 50 VP)
- Persons (versatile type some of them good for gold or resource production, some of them can generate VP, some of them can hinder the opponent etc. )
- Events (ongoing or instant conditions which can enhance your town or brake the opponent)

Buildings and Persons have 5 groups: 
-Worker [symbol: hammer]
-Scientiest [symbol: lighting bulb]
-Religous [symbol: cross]
-Leader [symbol: laurel wreath ]
-Merchant [symbol: three coins]

Anatomy of cards: 
- Indicator of group membership [Top left corner]
- Cost of the card [Top right corner]
- Illustartion [Center of the card]
- Ability of the card [Under the illustration]
- Production of the card [Bottom of the card] 
Gold | Common resource | Rare resource
[number of gold production] | [number of common resource production] | [number of rare resource production]

Resources:
- Gold
- Common resources (grey)
- Rare resources (purple)

Play area:
There are two areas of the playing area: Building and Townsfolks zone

##BUILDINGS##
Rules of the BUILDING ZONE:
There are three levels of building cards. The starter building cards are not parts of the starter deck. Each faction has 9 first level buildings, 6 second level buildings and 3 third level buildings. Choose 6 first level, 3 second level and 1 third level buildings and place them in the building zone face down except three of the first level buildings. These 3 first level building will be the starting pieces of your town. You can unlock the facedown building cards during the game when pay their costs.

The maximum number of 
    - 1st level buildings is 9
    - 2nd level buildings is 3
    - 3rd level building is 1 in the building zone. 

The player can add a new building in the given level until reaching the level limit OR replace a placed building from its hand with the following costs (it does not matter if a building's position is face-up or face-down):
    - 1 gold for each 1st level buildings
    - 3 gold for each 2nd level buildings
    - 6 gold for each 3rd level building.

Cost of the buildings:

You can reveal a face-down building card for the following costs:
- 1st level: 1 common resource + 1 additional common resource/face-up 1st level building
- 2nd level: 1 gold and 1 common resource + 1 gold and 1 common resource/face-up 2nd level building
- 3rd level: 2 gold, 2 common and 2 rare resource

Rules of the TOWNFOLKS ZONE:
The area near the building zone is your townfolks zone. You will be play persons here, who will generate more resources and utilize abilities in order to enrich your town.


##TOWNFOLKS##
You can as many persons as much capacity your town has. The capacity is derived from the buildings use the below measurement:
- 1 capacity/one 1st level building
- 2 capacity/one 2nd level building
- 3 capacity/the third level building

You can pay your person's cost with your capacity and to fulfill additional requirements.
 E.g. you have three 1st level and one 2nd level face-up building therefore you have 5 capacity. You can play 5 Miner (cost 1/Miner) or 2 Miner and 1 Supervisor miner (cost 3). You can not play the Miner Colony Overseerer (cost 5), although you have enough capacity (5) in order to pay its cost, however the Overseerer also has a requirement (You have to own a face-up 3rd level building). You do not have a face-up 3rd level building therefore this condition is not met.

##EVENTS##
As you are a the true leader of the designated town you have strong connections and even control mysterious powers (which can manipulate e.g. the weather), therefore the events can be a wide variety of possible actions, encounters and so on.
You can play events in the townfolks zone with its cost (gold and/or resources). The events can boost your production engine or obstacle your opponent.

The event cards are usually one-time cards, which means that they are moved to the discard pile after they are played and their effects were executed.

##SETUP##

Select the starting building cards (6+3+1) and place them face-down except three 1st level building in front of you. (Note: The sum of the buildings in the deck and the building zone can exceed the building zone limit's maximum values. Therefore you can add more buildings into your deck if you want, you can add the appropriate level of the building zone or replace already placed building during the game).
Shuffle your decks and draw 6 cards, if you would like you can mulligan only ONCE per game as many cards as you wish from this starting 6 cards, and take aside. Then draw as many card from your deck as you do not wanto keep then shuffle your deck and these cards.  
Decide the first player with dice throwing or other method and the first player can execute its turn.

##ROUND ORDER##
Upkeep phase:

- Activate any used building, person or event
- Draw 1 card from your deck

Activation phase(you can choose any of these actions as long as you can pay the cost or fullfil the requirement):

- Add or replace building cards in the building zone for the above mention costs
- Activate (rotate to 90 degree) an unused (not rotated and faced-down) building/person/event card in order to gain its production value/s or activate its abilities. The card's abilities also may have activation cost so you can only use them if you can pay their costs.
- Construct a building. Pay the building's cost and turn it to face-up and execute its ability if it has any. 
- Play a person or an event and execute its ability if it has any.
- Pass. If you can not or do not want to do anything else, your opponent countine the round with its Activation phase

Scoring phase:

If you both chose the “pass” action in succession.
- Sum up the number of VP-s which are collected in this round from cards which affect to you (building, persons and events)
- Check if one of the winning conditions is met. 
- If none of the winning condition is fullfiled play another round.
